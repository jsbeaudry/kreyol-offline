Depoze odyo oswa videyo isit la Drop audio or video files here, any length. Interviews, radio shows, sermons, meetings, YouTube videos.

    Done pou antrene Training data

    Klip odyo ak tèks yo, pare pou antrene yon modèl. The audio clips and their text as a 🤗 audiofolder dataset: 16 kHz WAV plus metadata.csv.

    Chwazi yon transkripsyon Ajoute yon odyo, oswa chwazi youn nan lis la pou w korije l. Add a recording, or pick one from the list to review and correct it.

    Klike sou lè a pou tande liy lan. ⌘/Ctrl+↵ verifye l epi tande pwochen an · ⌥/Alt+↵ tande l ankò · ↵ / ⇧+↵ pwochen / anvan. Click a time to hear its line. The shortcuts check a line and play the next, replay it, or move between lines. Changes save by themselves.

    Chwazi yon dosye

    Fichye yo rete kote yo ye: paj la li yo san l pa kopye yo. Files are read where they are, not copied.

      Dokiman

      tèks long → odyo documents to audio

      Yon leson, yon atik, yon anons: vwa a li tout tèks la epi ou jwenn yon sèl fichye odyo. A lesson, an article, an announcement: the voice reads all of it into one audio file you can share, play on the radio or put on a phone.

      .txt, .md, .docx, .csv

      CSV: yon fichye pou chak liy, pou meni telefòn oswa spot radyo. Kolòn: non,tèks,vwa. CSV makes one file per row (phone menus, radio spots). Columns: name, text, voice (optional).

      Bib la

      li ak koute read and listen

      Chwazi yon liv ak yon chapit. Peze sou nimewo yon liy pou tande depi la, oswa kite vwa a li tout chapit la pou ou. Pick a book and a chapter. Tap a line number to hear it from there, or let the voice read the whole chapter. The text and the voice are both on this computer.

      Li ak mwen

      li fò, machin nan koute reading practice

      Tande fraz la, li l fò, epi wè ki mo machin nan tande byen. Anrejistreman yo pa janm sove. Hear the sentence, read it aloud, and see which words came through. Recordings are never saved.

      mo byen li (heard) mo prèske (nearly) mo pa tande l (not heard)
        Vwa pou tande fraz la:

        Machin nan ka fè erè tou: si yon mo make an wouj men ou te li l byen, se petèt machin nan ki pa t tande l. Machine hearing makes mistakes too: a word marked red may have been read correctly. It learned from adult voices, so children's reading is harder for it.

        Reglaj
        
        
        jan zouti yo travay settings
        
      
        
      
        Si liy yo twò long epi modèl la kòmanse repete tèt li, kite mwens poz antre nan yon liy epi bese longè maksimòm nan.
        If lines come out too long and the model starts repeating itself, keep fewer pauses inside a line and lower the
          longest-line setting. Measured on five minutes of speech: with 2 s pauses kept and lines up to 28 s, 7.5% of the
          words were inside a repeated run; with 0.8 s and 20 s, none were.
        
      
        
      
        Modèl ki make ak yon ✓ se sa k ap sèvi kounye a. Klike sou yon lòt pou chanje l: paj la ap
        chaje l epi li p ap pran travay pandan tan sa a. Sa ki gen yon gwosè se sa ki poko sou machin nan.
        A ✓ marks the model in use. Clicking another loads it, and the page refuses work while it does.
          A size means it is not on this machine yet; clicking downloads it.
